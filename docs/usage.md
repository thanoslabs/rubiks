## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Turn a layer | Drag a sticker the way you want it to move. Edge and centre stickers on a middle row or column turn the slice. |
| Rotate the view | Drag anywhere off the cube |
| Keyboard | Letter = clockwise turn, **Shift** + letter = counter-clockwise (prime) |
| Buttons | One button per move in the control panel |
| Close the solved banner | **Escape** or **Close** |

## Notation

Standard [Singmaster/WCA notation](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/#article-12-notation), viewed with white on top and green in front:

| Move | Turns | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `U` `D` | Up / Down face | Clockwise as seen looking at that face |
| `L` `R` | Left / Right face | Clockwise as seen looking at that face |
| `F` `B` | Front / Back face | Clockwise as seen looking at that face |
| `M` | Middle slice (between L and R) | Same direction as `L` |
| `E` | Equator slice (between U and D) | Same direction as `D` |
| `S` | Standing slice (between F and B) | Same direction as `F` |
| `x` `y` `z` | Whole cube | Same direction as `R`, `U`, `F` |

Suffixes: `'` (also `’` or `′`) is counter-clockwise and `2` is a half turn (`2'` is accepted too). Wide moves (`Rw`, `r`) aren't supported.

## Scrambles

- **Random scramble** produces 25 face turns. The same face never turns twice in a row, and no three turns in a row are on the same axis (e.g. `R L R`).
- **Set scramble** applies any sequence you type or paste, starting from a solved cube. Invalid moves are reported with their position. A scramble that leaves the cube solved is rejected.

> [!info]
> Official WCA competition scrambles are *random-state*, generated by a solver. Random-move scrambles are the usual lightweight substitute.

## Timer and celebration

- The timer starts on your first turn after a scramble. Whole-cube rotations neither start it nor count as moves.
- When a scrambled cube is solved, you get confetti, a spin of the cube, and your move count and time. If your system asks for reduced motion, you get only the banner.
- Turning the cube without scrambling first is free play. It's never timed and never celebrated.

## Saved progress

The current attempt is saved in your browser's `localStorage` under the key `rubiks:v1`. It stores the moves since the cube was last solved, the scramble, the move count, and the elapsed time. Reloading the page restores it.

- The timer pauses while the page is closed and resumes on your next turn.
- **Reset** returns to a solved cube and clears the attempt.
- Saved data that is unreadable or inconsistent is ignored, and the page starts with a solved cube.
